Fat-Free Cunda Yoghurt (Cultured Buttermilk)

This product is produced after churning. Ghee settles and is removed. The liquid that remains is fat-free yoghurt. This improved traditional milk is good for both young and old people.

The protein found in this milk is called whey. It is more nutritious and has more bioactive compounds than casein (solid protein). Whey contains all the essential amino acids in the proper proportions, making it an excellent source of nutrition for therapeutic and supplementary foods.

Available in: Strawberry and Plain

Protein Drink

This drink is cherished by the youth who do bodybuilding and need it to tone their muscles, and for persons interested in losing excess body weight.

Ghee

Ghee contains a good amount of healthy fats, Vitamin A, E, and D. Many people think that the high content of fat in ghee is bad for health, but in fact, fat is one of the major nutrients required by our body to function properly.

Appetizer — Ready-to-Eat Ghee

This is a spiced delicacy, well made for school-going children (boarding schools).

Eshabwe

Eshabwe is a special cuisine made from ghee, treasured by people from western Uganda. It is now a treasured delicacy in most parts of the country during special occasions.

Amaranga (Whey)

This is the last by-product in the milk value chain. Although whey has huge potential for turning around the dairy industry, for now we sell it to pig farmers. It has the same nutritional value as amacunda, including being used in the preparation of different cuisines.
